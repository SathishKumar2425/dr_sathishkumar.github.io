01 / ABOUT

Building intelligent systems that understand visual data.

Dr. Sathish Kumar is a Computer Science academic and researcher with a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Rani Channamma University. His doctoral research focused on biometric data analysis for age and gender determination using digital image processing techniques.

His current research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Deep Learning, Biomedical Image Processing, Multimodal AI and Indic-Sign Language Translation.

He combines research, teaching and applied experimentation to develop robust intelligent systems for real-world visual and multimodal problems.
